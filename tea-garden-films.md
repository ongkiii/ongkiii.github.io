# Films, Documentaries & Shows About Tea Garden Workers

A comprehensive reference list for the research question:
> *How has the cinematic representation of tea workers evolved from colonial-era ethnographic films through nationalist cinema to contemporary parallel/independent cinema, and what continuities or ruptures exist across these periods?*

---

## Part I — Colonial & Early Ethnographic Films (Pre-1947)

| Title | Year | Type | Origin / Language | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Tea from Assam* (various British Pathé / Gaumont newsreels) | 1900s–1940s | Newsreel / Short | UK | Multiple short newsreel clips shot on Assam tea estates for British audiences; workers depicted as docile, picturesque labour. Available via British Pathé archive. |
| *Tea — From the Garden to the Cup* | c. 1903 | Short / Promotional | UK (filmed in Assam/Ceylon) | Early promotional film by the Tea Cess Committee; foregrounds the "exotic" plucking process, erases worker identity. |

| *Tea Cultivation in India* | 1936 | Documentary Short | India (British) | Films Division precursor; produced for colonial exhibition circuits. |
| *The Empire Tea Bureau Films* (multiple titles) | 1930s–1940s | Promotional / Documentary | UK / British India | Series of short films commissioned by the Empire Tea Bureau to promote Indian and Ceylonese tea to UK consumers; Assam and Darjeeling prominently featured. |

## Key Archives & Repositories for Primary Research

- **British Pathé Archive** (britishpathe.com) — largest repository of colonial newsreel footage from Indian tea estates (1895–1970)
- **BFI (British Film Institute) National Archive** — colonial documentary and ethnographic holdings
- **Films Division of India Archive** (filmsdivision.org) — documentary films post-1948, including several on Assam
- **National Film Archive of India (NFAI), Pune** — preserves Assamese and regional language films
- **Doordarshan Archives, Guwahati** — regional television productions on Assam tea gardens
- **ILO Film Archive** — labour-related documentaries including plantation workers
- **Al Jazeera Documentaries** (aljazeera.com/documentaries) — several investigative pieces freely available online
- **Journeyman Pictures** (journeyman.tv) — multiple tea-worker documentaries available online
